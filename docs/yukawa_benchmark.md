# Reproducing the published heterotic Yukawa tables

Before any Yukawa statement about our own candidates can be trusted, the engine
has to reproduce a published calculation. This records that benchmark: what was
compared, what agreed, what did not, and why.

The target is the Yukawa sector of the Anderson-Gray-Lukas-Palti standard-model
database (`sms202.m`, arXiv:1202.1757), which lists for each model the up-type
and down-type Yukawa matrices, the mu term, and the proton-decay and R-parity
operators. Section 3.1.6 of that paper states what the tables contain: for each
entry, the list of gauge-invariant monomials in the standard-model singlets
`S_{ab}`, with `{1}` for a term allowed at leading order and `{0}` for one that
is forbidden, *"where we have dropped terms higher than cubic"* in a given model.

## 1. Conventions, fixed by the comparison

None of these are deducible from the physics alone; each was established by
matching the published data and is recorded so the comparison is reproducible.

* `S_{ab}` carries `U(1)^4` charge `e_a - e_b`; `10_a` carries `e_a`;
  `5bar_{ab}` carries `e_a + e_b`; `5_{ab}` carries `-(e_a + e_b)`. Charges live
  in `Z^5 / Z(1,1,1,1,1)`.
* A coupling is allowed iff its total charge plus that of some monomial in the
  *available* singlets vanishes in the quotient. Since singlet charges have
  entry-sum zero, the multiple of `(1,1,1,1,1)` is forced by the entry sum.
* The Yukawa matrices use the *physical* Higgs pair, given per model by
  `PhysHiggs`, not an arbitrary Higgs in the spectrum.
* Families are ordered by increasing line-bundle index, repeated by
  multiplicity. This is not a guess: the per-model `EDownSpec` field encodes it
  and was decoded (see below).
* `Y_u` is indexed (10-family, 10-family) and is symmetric; `Y_d` is indexed
  (10-family, 5bar-family).
* Monomial lists include a valid monomial times any charge-neutral monomial, so
  the published lists are closed under multiplication by neutral factors.
* The published lists contain no monomial of total degree above five.

### Decoding `EDownSpec`

`EDownSpec[[1]]` is
`[H_u pair, three 10-family bundles, H_d pair, three 5bar-family pairs, one code
per singlet type]`, where a pair index is the position in the lexicographic list
of the ten unordered pairs and a singlet code is `2 * (pair index) + direction`,
direction `0` meaning `S_{ab}` with `a < b`. Checked against `DownSpec` on the
whole database; for example model 1 of X7862 has singlet codes
`0,2,4,6,9,11,13`, which decode to `S_{12}, S_{13}, S_{14}, S_{15}, S_{32},
S_{42}, S_{52}` -- exactly its listed singlets.

## 2. The selection rule, exhaustively

`physics/selection_rules.py` decides membership of a charge vector in the monoid
generated by the available `e_a - e_b`. That is a network-flow feasibility
question on the directed graph of available singlets, so it is decided exactly
and without any bound on the insertion order; the minimal order is a min-cost
flow. `enumerate_monomials` then lists every monomial up to a given degree,
pruned by the min-cost-flow lower bound, so the enumeration is complete.

Logical status: `FORBIDDEN_BY_SELECTION_RULE` is EXACT and proves the coupling
vanishes to all orders in singlet insertions. `ALLOWED_*` proves nothing about
the value -- that needs the cohomological product of Section 4.

**Result over the whole published database (2,012 models with complete records):**

| table | compared | monomial sets equal | allowed/forbidden differs |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `Y_u` | 2,012 | **2,012** | 1 |
| `Y_d` | 2,012 | **2,012** | 0 |
| mu term | 2,012 | **2,012** | 0 |

6,036 tables, every singlet monomial at every order, exact set equality. The one
flag difference is X7862 model 14, whose up-type coupling first becomes neutral
at insertion order six -- above the published truncation, where both lists are
empty and therefore still agree.

## 3. Entry by entry, and an inconsistency in the published `Y_d`

Set equality is weaker than cell-by-cell agreement, so the matrices were rebuilt
entry by entry on X7862:

| table | tables | exact | cells | cells agreeing |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `Y_u` | 22 | **22** | 198 | **198** |
| `Y_d` | 22 | 17 | 198 | 184 |
| mu term | 14 | **14** | 14 | **14** |

The five `Y_d` exceptions have a common structure, and it is not a convention
problem. Because the published tables are functions of the charges, two families
from the *same* line bundle must give identical rows. Testing that using only
published data -- the family order coming from the model's own `EDownSpec`, with
nothing computed here entering:

| table | checked | charge-consistent | internally inconsistent |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `Y_u` | 2,012 | **2,012** | 0 |
| `Y_d` | 2,007 | 1,053 | **954** |

Every published up-type table is consistent. Nearly half the down-type tables
are not: they contain a zero row where an identical row is nonzero. The five
X7862 tables we fail to reproduce are exactly five of those. Transposing the
table or exchanging the row and column labels does not repair it (1,028 and
1,028 inconsistent respectively), so it is not an indexing convention.

We do not know the cause and do not guess one. What can be said precisely is in
Section 4: for the two X7862 cases that are testable at leading order, the
cohomological product gives a matrix of rank two in the block where the
published table shows a zero row -- and a rank-two 2x3 matrix has no basis with
a zero row, so the published presentation cannot be recovered by any change of
family basis.

## 4. The cohomological product, where the charges are silent

At the Abelian locus, where the singlets have no vacuum expectation value, a
Yukawa entry is a genuine triple cup product, computed by
`physics/yukawa.equivariant_coupling`: the Koszul level of each class is
determined, the trilinear form is evaluated on Cech monomials, and the result is
restricted to the Gamma-isotypic components selected by the Wilson line.

**Self-test.** The trilinear form must be Gamma-covariant with the character of
`H^{top}(A, O(-q)) = prod_i det(B_i)^{-1}`, which is trivial exactly when the
action descends to the Calabi-Yau quotient. Checked element by element and index
triple by index triple: **zero violations**. That validates the Serre-duality
basis of `factor_action_matrix`, the exponent convention of `factor_exponents`,
the Kronecker ordering and the group lift simultaneously.

**Leading-order ranks against the published tables.** The published table gives
only which entries are allowed, so the fair comparison is our exact rank against
the published *structural* rank, the largest rank any matrix with that support
can have.

| table | models | ranks agree | differ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `Y_u` | 22 | **20** | 2 |
| `Y_d` | 22 | **20** | 2 |

* The two `Y_u` differences (models 15 and 16) are a genuine cohomological
  vanishing that charge conservation cannot see. All three classes sit at Koszul
  level 0, so the product is the restriction of a class in `H^3(A, O_A) = 0` and
  the coupling is identically zero. This is the vanishing rule of
  `physics/yukawa.py`, and it is exactly the phenomenon that makes a charge
  argument insufficient.
* The two `Y_d` differences (models 16 and 18) are the internally inconsistent
  published tables of Section 3.

**An independent confirmation.** Section 6.2 of arXiv:1202.1757 proves that,
without equivariant blocks, `Y_u` cannot have rank one: the coupling is
symmetric in family space while the Higgs charge `e_h + e_g` has `h < g`. Our
computed `Y_u` ranks across all 22 models are 0 or 2 and **never 1**, reproducing
that theorem from the cohomology rather than from the group theory.

## 5. Scope and limits

* Only the holomorphic superpotential. The overall normalisation of the trace
  map is not fixed, so only zero/nonzero patterns and ranks are meaningful.
* Physical, canonically normalised Yukawas need matter-field Kahler metrics,
  which are not computed here and are not claimed.
* The cohomological product is implemented for a single defining polynomial
  (one Koszul step) and for bundles whose `H^1(X, L)` sits at a single Koszul
  level; mixed-level cases are refused, not approximated.
* Couplings that need singlet insertions are treated at the level of the
  selection rule only. Their values involve the singlet vacuum and are not
  triple cup products.
* Everything is at a Zariski-generic member of the invariant defining family.

### Conventions established here

Each was found by a failing comparison, diagnosed, and then held fixed for the
whole database. Raw (convention-free) agreement is recorded alongside in the
artifacts so none of them is hidden.

1. **Truncation.** Superpotential lists stop at total operator degree eight,
   capped at singlet order five (three-field operators: order <= 5; the
   four-field `Dim5Proton`: order <= 4). Kahler lists stop at singlet degree three.
2. **Higgs slots.** `HiggsMass` is indexed by the three padded Higgs slots of
   `EDownSpec[[2]]` (alternating 5_H, 5bar_H pair indices; -1 = empty). Rows
   and columns of an empty slot vanish.
3. **`RParVio`** omits the bare (order-zero) bilinear: a quadratic term between
   massless fields is absent by construction (arXiv:1202.1757 Section 3.1.8).
   Raw agreement 5,876/6,036; the other 160 cells equal ours minus the bare term.
4. **`Dim5Proton`** is indexed 5bar-first, `lambda'_{s p q r}`, and lists an
   *inconsistent* subset of the permutations of the three 10 indices (for one
   index shape sometimes one ordering of three, sometimes two), so no fixed
   ordering convention exists. The charge is permutation-invariant; comparing
   per permutation class, every listed cell equals our list and a class is
   nonempty exactly when ours is.
5. **`Dim4Proton`** omits entries involving a matter 5bar from the same pair
   bundle as the physical down Higgs — charge-identical to a Yukawa coupling
   with H_d. No exceptions: all 19,450 allowed cells without such a 5bar are
   listed, all 164 with one (8 models, all on CICY 6770) are not.
6. **Kahler monomials** never contain a trivially neutral factor `S_{ab}
   S^dagger_{ab}` (with such factors: 94/2,012 GM tables, 0/2,012 `Kin5`
   tables; without: all exact).
7. **`Kin5`** is indexed `5bar^{p dagger} 5bar^q`.
8. **`NeutrinoKahler`** lists only monomials containing at least one
   holomorphic singlet — the one the paper uses as a right-handed neutrino.
9. **Physical Higgs.** Operators involving "the" Higgs use `PhysHiggs`, whose
   down-type member need not be the first Higgs slot. (An early version of our
   benchmark used the first slot and failed 8 of 22 GM tables on X7862; that was
   our bug, not a convention.)

## 7. Corrections and scope changes since the first version of this document

* **Koszul level sum two or more** is not a vanishing rule. It is now returned
  as `UNKNOWN_HIGHER_KOSZUL_LEVEL`. Audited: no recorded coupling had level sum
  two or more, so no number changed.
* **Nonzero Koszul maps.** A coupling whose ambient space is larger than
  `H^1(X, L)` is now refused (`UNKNOWN_NONZERO_KOSZUL_MAP`) instead of evaluated
  with a basis that would mis-index families. Audited: all 774 bundle slots ever
  evaluated had ambient space equal to `H^1(X, L)`, and every identification is
  *structural* — valid for every smooth member — so the leading-order matrices
  are independent of complex structure.
* **Equivariant structure.** Each slot now projects onto the Wilson-line
  character minus the equivariant structure of its bundle, the convention of
  `wilson_line.shift` that reproduces 1,948/1,948 published spectra. The earlier
  code ignored the shift. The 22 published X7862 models all have zero shift, so
  the benchmark above is unaffected; the candidate analysis was recomputed, with
  a blocking check that every slot's isotypic dimension equals the number of
  families the spectrum assigns to that bundle.
* **Speed.** For groups of exponent two the equivariant map ranks are now
  computed in exact rational arithmetic (`DomainMatrix` over QQ) rather than
  symbolically. Both paths give identical results on every bundle where both
  were run; on the largest singlet bundles (e.g. `O(4,-3,3,-4)`, ambient
  dimension 120) the symbolic path had not finished after more than an hour of
  CPU time, while the rational path takes under a second.
