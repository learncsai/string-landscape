index1 The List of Complete Intersection
Calabi-Yau Three-Folds This website holds the list of 7890 Calabi-Yau manifolds defined as
complete intersections in products of projective spaces, which was
first compiled in P. Candelas,
A.M. Dale, C.A. Lutken and R.
Schimmrigk, Nucl.Phys.B298 (1988) 493 . Our data contains the
Hodge numbers which were calculated in P.S. Green, T. Hubsch
and C. A. Lutken, Class.Quant.Grav. 6 (1989) 105 . The Mathematica file of the data
also holds the freely-acting discrete
symmetries which have been found by V. Braun
in arXiv:1003.3235 . Cicy three-fold data as a plain text file (2.3MB). Cicy three-fold data as a Mathematica file (6.1MB).
