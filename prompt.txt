# 0. SCIENTIFIC SCOPE

We consider the \(E_8\times E_8\) heterotic string compactified on a smooth Calabi–Yau threefold \(X\).

The observable gauge bundle is initially restricted to

$$
V=\bigoplus_{a=1}^{5} L_a
$$

with

$$
c_1(L_a)=k_a^iJ_i,
\qquad
k_a^i\in\mathbb Z,
$$

where

$$
i=1,\ldots,h^{1,1}(X).
$$

We require

$$
c_1(V)=\sum_{a=1}^{5}c_1(L_a)=0.
$$

The intended structure group is

$$
S(U(1)^5)\subset SU(5)\subset E_8,
$$

whose commutant produces an \(SU(5)\) GUT sector, together with Green–Schwarz massive \(U(1)\) factors.

A freely acting discrete symmetry

$$
\Gamma
$$

can subsequently be used to form

$$
\hat X=X/\Gamma
$$

and introduce Wilson lines breaking

$$
SU(5)\rightarrow
SU(3)\times SU(2)\times U(1)_Y.
$$

IMPORTANT TERMINOLOGY:

Do not call an upstairs \(SU(5)\) model an exact Standard Model.

Maintain the following explicit target hierarchy in the code:

1. `GUT_CHIRAL`

   * consistent heterotic line-bundle \(SU(5)\) compactification;
   * correct chiral asymmetry;
   * anomaly conditions;
   * appropriate stability requirement.

2. `GUT_FULL`

   * all `GUT_CHIRAL` conditions;
   * no \(\overline{\mathbf{10}}\) anti-families;
   * at least one vector-like \(\mathbf 5-\overline{\mathbf 5}\) Higgs pair;
   * appropriate spectrum conditions.

3. `DOWNSTAIRS_MSSM`

   * equivariant bundle under \(\Gamma\);
   * appropriate Wilson-line embedding;
   * Standard Model gauge group;
   * three chiral families;
   * Higgs doublet sector;
   * no charged chiral exotics;
   * correct Wilson-line projection.

4. `STRICT_NON_SUSY_SM`

   * RESERVED FOR FUTURE WORK.
   * Do not claim this has been constructed.
   * Calabi–Yau heterotic compactifications considered here produce an \(\mathcal N=1\) supersymmetric effective theory prior to SUSY breaking.

Our current scientific target is primarily `GUT_FULL`, followed later by `DOWNSTAIRS_MSSM`.

# 1. NON-NEGOTIABLE SCIENTIFIC RULES

These rules override convenience.

## Rule 1: exact arithmetic whenever possible

Quantities that are mathematically integral or rational must not silently be evaluated with floating-point arithmetic.

Use Python integers, rational arithmetic, SymPy rationals, or equivalent exact types.

Examples include:

* Chern classes;
* intersection numbers;
* Euler characteristics;
* line-bundle indices;
* discrete symmetry orders;
* candidate bundle matrices;
* anomaly inequalities when represented in an integral basis.

Floating-point calculations may be used for Kähler-moduli feasibility searches, but their status must be explicitly labeled.

---

## Rule 2: separate NECESSARY, SUFFICIENT, HEURISTIC and EXACT tests

Every constraint implementation must contain metadata:

```text
name
mathematical_status
reference
input_assumptions
can_safely_prune
can_certify_acceptance
```

Allowed statuses:

```text
EXACT
NECESSARY
SUFFICIENT
HEURISTIC
NUMERIC_WITNESS
UNKNOWN
```

A candidate may be permanently eliminated during an exhaustive search only if the failed condition is proven necessary under the assumptions of that search.

A heuristic may order candidates but MUST NEVER eliminate them from a supposedly exhaustive search.

---

## Rule 3: never confuse bounded exhaustion with an unbounded theorem

If we exhaust

$$
|k_a^i|\le K
$$

and find no model, report

```text
UNSAT_BOUNDED(K)
```

and not

```text
UNSAT
```

for the unrestricted problem.

If models stop appearing as \(K\) increases, report

```text
EMPIRICAL_SATURATION
```

unless an independent theorem has established that no solution can exist beyond that \(K\).

Never convert empirical saturation into a no-go theorem.

---

## Rule 4: a numerical failure is not a proof

For example, if an optimizer fails to find Kähler moduli satisfying the slope-zero equations, that is not by itself grounds for exact rejection.

Return

```text
UNKNOWN_STABILITY
```

unless a mathematically sound necessary condition is violated.

Likewise, a numerical root constitutes a candidate witness, not an exact certificate unless validated with certified methods.

---

## Rule 5: every important result must be independently testable

Implement a slow reference method for tiny search spaces.

Implement the optimized method separately.

On every domain for which brute force is affordable:

$$
\text{solutions}_{\rm brute}
=
\text{solutions}_{\rm optimized}
$$

must hold exactly as sets after canonicalization.

No optimized enumerator may be trusted before passing differential tests against the reference enumerator.
